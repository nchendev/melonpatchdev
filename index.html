Melon Patch is a small independent studio founded in 2026, making games and software. Its first game is TUG Co., a calm puzzle game for iOS and Android. In TUG Co. you tug rows left and right to bring every keystone home. Some rows move together and others move against one another. It holds more than 80,000 free puzzles, all openable by playing, three new Daily puzzles arrive every day, and there are no timers and no fail states. Every puzzle mode plays fully offline and no account is required.

FAQ

What is Melon Patch?

+

Melon Patch is a small independent studio making games and software. Founded in 2026. The philosophy: ship small, iterate fast, care deeply about the people who use what we make.

What is TUG Co.?

+

TUG Co. is a calm puzzle game for iOS and Android. You tug rows left and right to bring every keystone home. Some rows move together, others move against one another. It holds more than 80,000 free puzzles, three new Daily puzzles arrive every day, and there are no timers and no fail states. Every puzzle mode plays fully offline and no account is required. More about TUG Co. →

Is TUG Co. free?

+

Yes, the game and every puzzle in it. The Base campaign is open from the start, and the Heavy Gear pack is earned and opened with Gold you get from playing. Real money only buys Gold faster; no puzzle ever requires it.
