Description

TUG Co. is a calm puzzle game presented as the player's first day at an unusually cheerful company. Tug rows left and right to bring every keystone home. Some rows move together. Others move against one another. Learn how each puzzle is connected, find the right sequence, and let the crew handle the pulling.

It holds more than 80,000 puzzles across the Base campaign and the Heavy Gear pack, from simple introductions to elaborate networks of linked movement, and every one of them can be opened by playing. Three new Daily puzzles arrive every day in Normal, Hard, and Hell difficulties. There are no timers and no fail states, and every puzzle mode plays fully offline. No account required.

History

TUG Co. began as a puzzle generator: tooling that could build coupled-row puzzles by the tens of thousands and grade each one for difficulty. The game grew around what that made possible: a campaign far deeper than anyone is expected to finish, wrapped in a small workplace fiction where a cheerful crew does the pulling.

It is Melon Patch's first game.

Features

  • More than 80,000 puzzles, all free to open by playing
  • Three new Daily puzzles every day
  • No timers or fail states; undo and restart freely
  • Every puzzle mode playable offline
  • No account required; progress stays on the device
  • Text size up to 200%, UI scaling, reduced motion, and cues that do not rely on color alone
