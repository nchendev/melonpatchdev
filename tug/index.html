TUG Co. is a calm puzzle game for iOS and Android by Melon Patch LLC. You tug rows left and right to bring every keystone home. Some rows move together and others move against one another. The game holds more than 80,000 puzzles across the Base campaign and the Heavy Gear pack, and every one of them can be opened by playing; real money only ever buys Gold, the in-game currency, faster. Three new Daily puzzles arrive every day in Normal, Hard, and Hell difficulties. There are no timers and no fail states, and you can undo and restart freely. Every puzzle mode plays fully offline, including the campaign, Daily puzzles, Practice, and hints. No account is required. Progress is stored on your device. Accessibility features include text size up to 200 percent, UI scaling, reduced motion support, and visual cues that do not rely on color alone.

Questions

Is TUG Co. free?

Yes, the game and every puzzle in it. The Base campaign is open from the start, and the Heavy Gear pack is earned and opened with Gold you get from playing. Real money only buys Gold faster; no puzzle ever requires it.

Does TUG Co. have ads?

TUG Co. shows no ads. There are no banner ads, no video ads between puzzles, and no interruptions.

Can you play TUG Co. without internet?

Yes. Every puzzle mode plays fully offline: the Base campaign, the three Daily puzzles, Practice, and hints. Only purchasing needs a connection.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no sign-in. Progress and Gold are stored on your device. They do not sync between devices, so keep a device backup if your progress matters to you.

Is there a time limit on puzzles?

No. There are no timers, no move limits that fail you, and no fail states of any kind. You can undo, restart, and think as long as you like.
