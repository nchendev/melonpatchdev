01

Brand and values

Minimal and editorial. Drawn from print: generous whitespace, careful hierarchy, restrained color. Departures are on purpose: brutalist card hovers, organic blobs, and dark mode as a first-class experience.

Value

Substance over style

Every product, feature, and piece of content should justify its existence. No filler.

Value

Clarity

In code, in design, in communication. Say what you mean, then stop.

Value

Craft

Sweat the details. Good spacing, good color, good type. These things matter.

Value

Independence

We own our platform, our audience, and our decisions.

Usage rules

MELONPATCH

Clear space = height of "M" on all sides

  • Always use Inter 800. Do not substitute fonts.
  • Keep the -0.02em tracking. Compact, not loose.

05

Spacing and layout

An 8px base grid. Generous whitespace is non-negotiable: 128px between major sections. 32px horizontal padding at every breakpoint.

Spacing scale

Content widths

1280Full · main container
768Narrow · blog, FAQ
672Tight · CTA, forms
576Column · hero text

Grids

  • Dense grids (features, specs): 24px gap.
  • Spacious grids (products, team): 32px gap.
  • Single column on mobile. Two columns from ~768px. Three from ~1024px.
06

Dark mode

Not a dimmed light mode. A parallel design with green-tinted surfaces that create depth without shadows. Never pure black, never generic dark gray.

#0f1612 · Ground

Dark Background

Page background, base layer.

#1a241e · Surface

Dark Surface

Cards, panels, elevated UI.

#253029 · Elevated

Dark Elevated

Popovers, layered cards.

Light to dark mapping

White surface→Dark Surface Off-white surface→Dark Background Kaitoke accents→Moon Mist accents Gray 900 headings→Moon Mist headings Gray 600 body→Gray 400 body

Rules

  • Alternate sections between Background and Surface.
  • Borders: Moon Mist at 10%, full Moon Mist on hover.
  • Shadows: Moon Mist RGBA at 4–5%. Heavy shadows look wrong.
  • Kaitoke Green still works for filled buttons and active states.

Tip: use the theme toggle in the top bar to see this whole page switch.

A smart friend who builds software. Not a corporation. Not a lifestyle brand. Not a hype machine.

Writing rules

  1. Short sentences. More than one comma? Consider splitting.
  2. No jargon unless the audience shares it.
  3. No filler. Every sentence carries weight.
  4. Avoid em dashes. Use periods or commas instead.
  5. No emojis in written content.
  6. Active voice. "We built this."
  7. Say "we", not "the team at MelonPatch."
  8. Sentence case headings. Not Title Case.
