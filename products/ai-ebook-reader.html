MelonReader

Read the books you want. Learn the language as you go.

MelonReader is an iOS ebook reader built for language learners. Bring any EPUB or TXT book — or start from a curated public-domain library — and read in the language you're learning. Tap any word or phrase for an instant translation in your native language, hear how it sounds, and turn what you read into vocabulary you actually remember.

Private by design: your books, words, and progress stay on your device. Free to start — MelonReader Plus unlocks unlimited books.

01

Features

T

Tap to Translate

Tap any word or phrase while reading for an instant translation in your native language, right there on the page. No copy-pasting into another app.

C

Context That Fits

Translations read the surrounding sentence, so you get the meaning that fits the passage you're reading — not just a dictionary definition out of context.

P

Hear It Spoken

Hear any word or phrase read aloud, with pronunciation readings like pinyin and kana from optional dictionary packs. Learn how it actually sounds.

W

Your Word Bank

Save words and phrases as you read. They're collected in a personal word bank, organized by language — and you can export it to CSV anytime.

R

Spaced-Repetition Review

Turn saved words into built-in flashcards, with separate decks per language. Spaced repetition brings them back at the right time so they stick.

B

Bring Any Book

Import your own EPUB or TXT files in any language, or start from a curated public-domain library. Read what you actually want to read — every book you bring stays yours.

02

Private by Design

Your reading is yours. MelonReader is local-first — your books, saved words, and progress live on your device, not on our servers.

There's no account and no sign-in. Translation runs on-device, so the words and passages you tap aren't sent off to be translated. We collect only anonymous, optional usage data to fix bugs and improve the app — never your books or the words you read. Read the privacy policy →

Storage

On your device. No account required.

Translation

On-device. Your reading stays private.

Tracking

No ads. No data sold. Ever.

03

How It Works

1

Bring a Book You Want to Read

Import any EPUB or TXT file, or pick something from the curated public-domain library. Japanese, Spanish, French, Korean, Mandarin — read whatever you're learning, at the level you enjoy.

2

Tap to Understand

Stuck on a word or phrase? Tap it for an instant translation in your native language — one that reads the surrounding sentence for context — and hear how it's pronounced. Stay in the story instead of reaching for a dictionary.

3

Save and Remember

Save new words to your word bank as you go, then review them with spaced-repetition flashcards. The vocabulary you meet in your books becomes vocabulary you keep.
